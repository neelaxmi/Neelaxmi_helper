JEE Previous Year Questions (PYQs) help you understand how concepts appear in the actual exam. Practise chapter-wise, review your mistakes and use each attempt to plan your next revision.

Read the full guide

Why are JEE Main PYQs useful?

Previous-year questions are a practical way to connect what you study with the style of questions asked in JEE Main. They let you practise applying concepts, rather than only reading theory or memorising formulas. Reviewing questions from different years can also help you become familiar with the exam's format and level of application.

How to use chapter-wise practice

Choose a chapter you have studied and attempt its questions without checking the solution immediately. After the quiz, review every incorrect or guessed answer. Note whether the issue was a concept gap, a calculation error, a misread question or time management. Revise that topic and try another practice set later.

Build a consistent revision routine

Short, focused practice sessions can make revision easier to organise. Keep track of chapters that need more attention, revisit important concepts regularly and gradually mix questions from completed chapters. Accuracy and clear understanding matter more than rushing through a large number of questions.

Use practice results thoughtfully

A quiz score is a snapshot of one attempt, not a prediction or guarantee of an exam result. Use it to identify what you know and what you should revise next. Combine PYQ practice with your syllabus, textbooks, class notes and a balanced study plan.
