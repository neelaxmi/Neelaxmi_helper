About NEELAXMI

More Than A Learning Platform.

NEELAXMI is an educational initiative built around the idea of connected learning.

Our goal is to bring educational resources, practice opportunities and digital learning experiences together in one unified ecosystem.

We believe that students should have access to organised, accessible and useful learning resources that help them move forward with confidence.

Our Story

NEELAXMI began with a simple idea: learning works best when everything a student needs is connected. Questions, quizzes, notes and updates shouldn't live in scattered places — they belong together. That idea is what THE UNION stands for.

Our Vision

To build a connected educational ecosystem that empowers students through technology and accessible learning experiences.

Our Mission

To make digital learning resources more organised, accessible and useful for students.

Our Philosophy

Learning should be continuous, accessible and collaborative.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

NEELAXMI — THE UNION is a connected educational ecosystem that brings questions, quizzes, learning resources and student-focused tools together in one destination.

The Question Universe is NEELAXMI's large collection of educational questions, organised for subject-wise, chapter-wise and topic-based practice and revision.

The collection currently stands at 200,000+ questions, and it continues to grow.

The Question Universe covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics.

NEELAXMI is built for students who want to practise, revise and improve their knowledge with organised, accessible resources.

The ecosystem includes the Question Universe, Quiz Arena, learning resources and study materials, a Student Dashboard, Mindmaps & Notes, and Class Updates.

Use the contact form or the email and social links in the Contact section. We welcome enquiries, collaborations and feedback.
