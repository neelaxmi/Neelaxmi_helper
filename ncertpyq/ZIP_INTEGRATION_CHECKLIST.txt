NCERT PYQ ZIP integration — status

Structure
- Front-end files are under ncertpyq/public/ to match public/ in pyq.zip.
- Netlify functions are under ncertpyq/netlify/functions/.
- Shared function libraries are under ncertpyq/netlify/functions/lib/.
- Root package.json, package-lock.json, readme.md and netlify.toml stay in ncertpyq/.
- ncertpyq/index.html redirects to /ncertpyq/public/index.html so the app is served from the same Vercel project.
- The root Netlify config uses publish = public and functions = netlify/functions.
- The separate pyq/ pages were renamed to pyqncert/ and site links/sitemap were updated.
