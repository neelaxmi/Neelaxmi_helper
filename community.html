Community Guidelines

Welcome to neelaxmi the union!

We are dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment for everyone. To ensure our community remains a positive space, we ask all members to adhere to the following guidelines.

1. Respect and Inclusivity

  • Treat all members with respect and kindness.
  • Harassment, bullying, and discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Be mindful of diverse perspectives and engage in constructive conversations.

2. Prohibited Content

  • Hate Speech: Content that promotes violence or incites hatred against individuals or groups based on attributes such as race, religion, ethnic origin, sexual orientation, disability, or gender.
  • Spam and Self-Promotion: Repeatedly posting repetitive content, unsolicited advertisements, or irrelevant links.
  • Illegal Content: Do not share content that violates any local, national, or international laws.

3. Privacy and Safety

  • Do not share personal, private, or sensitive information about yourself or others (e.g., home addresses, phone numbers).
  • Report any suspicious or harmful behavior to the administration team immediately.

4. Consequences of Violation

Violations of these guidelines may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent banning from the community, at the discretion of the moderation team.

5. Contact the Moderation Team

If you have any questions or need to report a violation, please reach out to us at: neelaxmi.support@gmail.com.
